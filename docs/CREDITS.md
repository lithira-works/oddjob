# Picture credits

These pictures are stand-ins that show the world of each trade. None shows an Oddjob customer, worker or job. They are resized and cropped. Four also have mild sharpening and colour adjustment, noted in the Changes line below. No picture has generated content. The site shows the same list at `/credits`.

**Changes:** `leak.jpg` and `roof.jpg` are resized, sharpened and colour adjusted. `fence.jpg` and `tree.jpg` are also cropped: the fence to remove a company's sign and phone number, the tree to leave out people and a cable. `leak.jpg` and `tree.jpg` are adaptations shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, the same licence as their originals.

Category icons are from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), ISC licence, unchanged. They are drawn in code, so they have no picture files in the list below.
