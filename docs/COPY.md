# Copy

## Voice

Plain and a little dry, like a capable neighbour. Short words. Specific nouns. Every line should be impossible to reuse for another company.

- Good: "Say what's wrong. It takes less time than finding a bucket."
- Bad: "Connecting you with trusted professionals seamlessly."

## Rules

- Sentence case. Display type turns uppercase through CSS, so write it normally.
- Headlines are about 32 characters or fewer, and two lines at most.
- One primary action name everywhere: **Find someone**.
- Say "people" or "pros", never "vendors" or "partners".
- Say "credits" and show the number before anyone spends.
- Say "up to five people", never "unlimited quotes".
- Avoid: unlock, seamless, effortless, elevate, empower, supercharge, trusted, world-class, reimagined.
- Never invent proof: no customers, quotes, ratings, press or counts.
- Use curly quotes and apostrophes in the interface.

## Emergencies

Safety wording comes before any sale. Gas, fire and electric shock wording sends people to emergency services first. Wording stays general until Sri Lankan numbers are confirmed with official sources. A wrong number here is worse than none.
